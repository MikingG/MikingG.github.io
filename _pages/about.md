I am a PhD student in computer science in the University of Melbourne, supervised by Prof. [Bach Le](https://xuanbachle.github.io/) and Prof. [Cezary Kaliszyk](https://ckaliszyk.github.io/). Before that, I completed my B.E. in Software Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University in June 2025, supervised by Prof. [Yanlin Wang](https://yanlin.info/) and Prof. [Zibin Zheng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=zWnsNrkAAAAJ&hl=en).

My research interests lie in AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE). In particular, I work on specification generation for verifying agentic AI systems.

Collaborations are very welcome.
